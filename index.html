Give generously. Stay on budget.

GiftingSmart is a free gift planner and budget tracker. Plan gifts for any occasion, set a budget for the whole event or for each person, and watch your spending as you shop. We track prices on your list and tell you when something goes on sale.

How it works

  1. Sign in with Google. Your events and lists are private to your account.
  2. Create an event, such as a birthday, Christmas, Hanukkah, Eid al-Fitr, Diwali, Lunar New Year, a baby shower, a wedding or a housewarming.
  3. Set one budget for the event, or a budget for each person you're buying for.
  4. Add gift ideas with links. Mark each one bought with what you paid, and get a warning before you go over budget.
