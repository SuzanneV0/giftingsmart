Cookie policy

Effective {{updated}}

The short version: the only cookies are optional Google Analytics cookies, and they're set only if you click Accept on the cookie banner. No advertising cookies. The app also keeps a few items in your browser's local storage so it works. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings in the footer.

Optional analytics cookies

If you accept, Google Analytics sets these cookies to count visits and see which pages are used. Nothing is loaded from Google, and no cookies are set, until you accept.

NameSet byPurposeHow long
_gaGoogle AnalyticsTells visits from the same browser apart, without identifying you.2 years

Google Analytics receives the pages you view, approximate location (derived from your IP address), device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site. We never send it your name, email, or the names of your events, gifts or people. Google processes this data under Google's privacy policy, and our account has Google's advertising features turned off. Choosing Decline later removes these cookies.

These items are strictly necessary for features you ask for, so they don't require consent under Canadian privacy law. Fonts and app code are served from our own site, so unless you accept analytics cookies, no third party learns that you visited.

Signing in with Google

When you choose "Continue with Google", you go to Google's sign-in page, where Google may use its own cookies under Google's privacy policy. That happens only when you sign in.

Removing stored items

  • Signing out removes your session.
  • You can clear everything at any time in your browser's settings (look for "site data" or "cookies and site data").
  • Blocking site storage will stop you from staying signed in.
